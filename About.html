Annotated Sequences

The text of the book is unchanged. What this edition adds is a skeptic's annotation overlay: 2,098 margin notes on 337 of the essays, each sitting beside the passage it is about. Where an essay cites a study, the note says what the study actually found, whether it replicated, and what later work concluded. Questionable reasoning is also called out.

Currently all annotations have been provided by AI, but this may well be expanded in the future to include human argumentation. Some of these agents were told to be skeptical, others to be supportive.

The notes come in eight independent passes, and you choose which you see from the panel in the top right corner of any page:

  • Opus — the original skeptical pass over the essays with citable empirical claims, all six books.
  • Fable — a deliberately adversarial pass covering almost every essay in the book, philosophical ones included.
  • Rationalist Opus and Rationalist Astra — tasked with defending against Fable's skepticism, they supply a rebuttal.
  • Old Rationalist Opus — the first Rationalist Opus rebuttal of Book I, since replaced by a new pass that answers every Fable note there.
  • Skeptic Astra — a second skeptical pass over Book VI.
  • Opus 5.5 — a maximum-skepticism pass over all of Book VI, which replaces the earlier Opus notes there; those remain available as Opus (old).

If you'd like the summation:

  • Fable's takedown of Book I / Opus' Rebuttal / the older Opus rebuttal
  • Fable's takedown of Book II / Opus' Rebuttal
  • Fable's takedown of Book III
  • Fable's takedown of Book IV
  • Opus 5.5's takedown of Book V
  • Opus 5.5's takedown of Book VI / Astra's / the older Opus pass

The annotations are not endorsed by the author or the publisher, and any errors in them are the annotators' own.
